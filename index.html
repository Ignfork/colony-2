Questions

Does Colony keep any of the fees?

No. Every creator fee goes to the coin's holders. The only cost is the Solana network fee of each transaction.

Who gets paid?

Every wallet holding at least 0.2 SOL worth of the coin. Shares are weighted by amount held, seconds held, loyalty and the wearing bonus.

Can the creator change the fee sharing later?

No. The launch sets pump.fun fee sharing to 100 percent for the Colony payout wallet and locks it. Nobody can change it afterwards, including the creator and us.

What happens to my loyalty if I buy or sell?

Buying more mixes new coins into your bag, so the age drops in proportion. Selling part cuts the age by the part you sold. Selling everything starts you again from 1x.

How is wearing the coin checked?

Your pump.fun picture counts 1.25x, your X picture counts 1.25x once you sign in with X and link your wallet, and both count 1.5x. Every holder is checked again every 30 minutes.

What is an outpost?

A child of a colony, paired with its parent on pump.fun so every buy of the outpost buys the parent first. Its 0.95 percent creator fee goes to its holders in the parent's coin.

Where is the $Colony contract address?

Only on our X account, @ColonySol. Any address posted anywhere else is not ours.

How it works

Coins grow when colonies do

Colony is a launchpad for community coins on pump.fun. Every fee a coin earns goes to the people who hold it.

1

Launch

You launch from your own wallet. The launch sets the coin's pump.fun fee sharing to 100 percent for the Colony payout wallet and locks it, so it can never be changed.

The coin is listed with its own picture on the front page and in Colonies. The three biggest stand on the podium.

2

Every fee to the holders

Every 3 minutes the payout desk collects the coin's creator fees and pays them out in SOL to every wallet holding at least 0.2 SOL worth of the coin. Nothing is kept by the platform. The only cost is the Solana network fee of each transaction.

share = amount held × seconds held × loyalty × wearing bonus
3

Loyalty, up to 4x

A fresh colonist counts 1x. The longer you hold, the more you count. The climb is fastest in the first weeks and gentler every day after, towards 4x. By day 100 the line is almost flat.

The age belongs to your coins. Buying more mixes new coins into your bag, so the age drops in proportion. Selling part of your bag cuts the age by the part you sold. Selling everything starts you again from 1x.

loyalty = 1 + 3 × (1 − e^(−days held / 20))
1 day 1.15x10 days 2.18x30 days 3.33x100 days 3.98x
4

Wear the coin

Colonists who wear the coin as their profile picture are the ones working its bags, so they earn more. Wearing counts two ways: the coin's own picture, or a picture that follows the colony's look, written at launch. If the look is "any animal wearing a hoodie", a zebra in a hoodie on pink counts just like the dog in a hoodie on yellow the coin started with.

Your pump.fun profile picture counts 1.25x. Your X profile picture counts 1.25x once you sign in with X and link your wallet. Both together count 1.5x. Every holder is checked again every 30 minutes, and the bonus ends when the picture comes off.

5

Outposts

Anyone can launch an outpost of a colony. An outpost is paired with its parent on pump.fun, so every buy of the outpost buys the parent first. The outpost is listed on its parent's page.

An outpost carries its own 0.95 percent creator fee, paid in the parent's coin. All of it goes to the outpost's holders in the parent's coin, unsold, as their reward for pumping the parent. Each holder decides whether to keep it or sell it. The only tokens sold are the few that pay Solana network fees and the rent of a new token account for a holder who has none yet, taken from that holder's own share.

6

Every payout on the ledger

Every collection and every payout is written to a public ledger with its Solana transaction. Open the ledger.
